"# JUEGO-_UMBRA" 

UMBRA is a browser-based playable demo and a collection of visual screen prototypes.

## Play

Open [jugar.html](./jugar.html) in a browser, or visit the deployed [GitHub Pages demo](https://luismendoza04062011-ship-it.github.io/JUEGO-_UMBRA/jugar.html).

Collect all five lumens and reach the portal. Defeat creatures to restore health and energy.

- Move: `A` / `D` or the arrow keys
- Jump and double jump: `Space`, `W`, or `Up`
- Crystal sword: `J`
- Ancestral flash: `K`
- Shadow dash: `Shift`
- Pause: `Escape`
- Restart: `R`

Touch controls are available in mobile browsers. This is an early playable prototype, not the complete game."
